Our story

A problem
close to home.

Two founders. A shared reason to begin.

Why should anyone have to choose between comfort, safety and their identity?

For Malasa, it started with her younger sister’s experience of discomfort and a cut while securing her hijab with goggles. For Rida, it was the difficulty of keeping her hijab secure and comfortable during a chemistry lab.

Those experiences inspired ModuSafe. We’re starting with the needs of hijab-wearing students and professionals in healthcare and labs.

A few things to know.

Can I buy a ModuSafe hijab yet?

We’re still refining the design and developing the prototype. Leave your details below if you’d like to express interest or receive future updates.

Is ModuSafe safety-certified?

We do not currently claim safety certification. Product performance and compatibility with specific PPE need validation. Always follow your workplace or institution’s PPE requirements.
